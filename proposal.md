# MASCOT: Disruptive mHealth Companion for Student Autonomy

**Category**: Youth Sexual & Reproductive Health (SRH) · **Platform**: Android & iOS (Offline-First)

---

### The Problem
University students in Zimbabwe face high rates of unplanned pregnancies and STI/HIV transmissions due to low contraception adherence and social stigma. Fear of peer surveillance in student hostels and high mobile data costs prevent youth from accessing standard SRH applications, creating a critical gap in reproductive autonomy.

### The Solution: MASCOT
**MASCOT** (Mobile-based Anonymous Self-care Companion) is a trilingual (English, Shona, Ndebele), offline-first mHealth companion designed specifically for student hostel environments. Built to eliminate stigma and data barriers, it offers confidential self-care tools directly on-device.

### What is Already Built (Implemented MVP Features)
1. **Dynamic Contraceptive Method Matcher**: Personalized algorithmic engine recommending appropriate methods based on user lifestyle, safety concerns, and medication history.
2. **Offline Cycle & Fertility Tracker**: Circular cycle wheels charting menstruation, follicular, and fertile phases, completely offline without remote servers.
3. **Discreet Notification Masker (Anti-Stigma Cover)**: Shields users by masking reminders under harmless Daily Duolingo lessons, System Warns, or Battery Alerts to prevent hostel peer surveillance.
4. **Offline SOS Emergency Center**: Pulsing dashboard warning card linking to an interactive 72-hour PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis) timeline and direct, toll-free dialers for Musasa and CeSHHAR.
5. **Private PIN & Biometric Lock**: Round 10-key keypad and FaceID/TouchID bypass with automated background screen locking to ensure roommate-proof privacy.
6. **AI Chatbot & Streamlined Assessment**: Local trilingual rule-based advisor and a stress-free 5-question risk diagnostic with instant offboarding for sexually inactive students.

### Next-Gen Features (To Stand Out & Win)
To secure a winning slot, we propose three disruptive expansions leveraging MASCOT’s offline local architecture:

#### 1. Gamified Offline Micro-Learning (Learn2Earn)
Students take interactive 60-second quiz modules on SRH. Upon completion, the app generates a cryptographically signed offline token that can be redeemed at university student clinics for actual **airtime or mobile data bundles** sponsored by corporate partners (Econet/NetOne), directly solving the digital divide.

#### 2. Peer-Led Smart Referral Vouchers
An anonymous, token-based referral system. Students can generate unique offline QR voucher codes within the app to share with friends. When a peer redeems the voucher at a partner clinic (e.g., CeSHHAR), both students receive anonymous reward points, accelerating youth peer-to-peer mobilization.

#### 3. Anti-Counterfeit Pill-Pack Verification
An offline scanner that utilizes the device camera to read QR codes on contraceptive pill packs. It cryptographically authenticates the medicine local database to verify batch legitimacy, shielding students from counterfeit black-market pills common in student communities.
